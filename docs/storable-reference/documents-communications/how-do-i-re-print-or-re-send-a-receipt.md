# How do I re-print or re-send a receipt?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15696482048535-How-do-I-re-print-or-re-send-a-receipt
**Article ID:** 15696482048535

---

Follow the instructions below if you would like to reprint or resend(email/text) a receipt for a customer.

1\. Hover over the _Customers_ menu and click **All Customers.** Click the customer's name from the customer list. | ![all](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/15696768928023)  
---|---  
3\. Click on the date to the left of the payment of the receipt under _Billing History._ | ![billing](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/15696768930199)  
4\. Click on the **Receipt** button in the top right corner.  
5\. Click on PDF and then download the PDF.  
  
**If you do not see the option to email or text the receipt:**

1\. Go to Email, Txt & Print > Templates.

2\. Click on **Manually Sent Payment Receipt** (under automatic templates).

3\. Check **email enabled** and **text enabled** then save.

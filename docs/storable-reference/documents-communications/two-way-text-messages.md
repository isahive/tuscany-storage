# Two-Way Text Messages

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18031042253591-Two-Way-Text-Messages
**Article ID:** 18031042253591

---

#### ⚠️ This article applies to customers in the United States and Canada.

## Enable two-way texting

To enable Two-Way Text Messaging, hover over the  _Email, Txt & Print_ tab and click **Text Messages**.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/648b4b181211660f0912dcc3/file-HdOvaInpEx.png)

Click **Enable Two-way texting** and enter an area code to see a list of phone numbers to choose from.

**You will then need to register your Two-Way texting number.** Please refer to [THIS ARTICLE](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/20061697455639-Registering-your-SMS-phone-numbers-for-Two-Way-Texting) for further information and instructions on how to register your Two-way texting number. 

## Send a message

Once your brand has been approved and your two-way texting number is active, you will be able to send text messages to your tenants and utilize the [text-to-open feature](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14012079584279-Cloud-Access-Control-Enable-Text-to-Open) with the Access Control gate system.

To send a text message, go to the customers account page and click the blue **Text Message** button next to the tenant's cell phone number. Type your message and attach a file if you would like to. Then click **Send**. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cd9edbb2c7d3a177d6eb67c/file-tyhI5p1RlH.gif)

If you get a response from the customer, you will see a red number icon at the top of the screen on the _Email, Txt & Print_ tab. Click on this to see the new response.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cd9efb504286306738f0d4a/file-JcHxR6gmZu.gif)

## Delete a message

Managers can delete text messages sent by tenants. This can be helpful if a tenant sends sensitive information that you do not want to be kept in the text message thread.

1\. When hovering over a text message, a Trash Can icon will appear. Click the trash can to delete the message.

![delete.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29609809115927)

2\. Click **Ok** from the confirmation pop up message.  
![delete message.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29609809118999)

3\. Once the message has been deleted, a message stating that the message was deleted will be displayed in its place.

![deleted.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29609809120791)

## Text message notifications

By default, our email notification service will alert you when your facility receives an inbound text message. This makes it easy to view and respond to text messages when you aren’t in front of your software. 

The text message will be included in the email. You can tap on the **Customer page** link to go straight to the tenant record and reply directly to the tenant.

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31594809066135)

The feature is enabled by default if you use Two-Way Texting, but can be disabled in your software settings by hovering over **Email, Txt, & Print** and clicking **Settings**.

![ext settings.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31594823293719)

Uncheck **Inbound Text** under the _Emailed Notifications_ section and click**Update Communication Settings.**

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31594809067927)

## Text message restrictions

**SMS registration**

Your brand and phone number/s used to text customers must be registered. Mobile carriers will block text messages that are sent from unregistered business phone numbers. You can read more about this requirement and the registration in our article: [Registering your SMS phone numbers.](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/20061697455639-Registering-your-SMS-phone-numbers-for-Two-Way-Texting)

**Prohibited text message content**

There are a number of messaging use cases that are not allowed in the United States or Canada. Storable Easy users are prohibited from using Storable Easy SMS capabilities for these activities. You can view more details and a list of prohibited messaging categories that apply to long code messaging (A2P 10DLC) and Toll-Free messaging on our [messaging partner's website.](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045004974-Forbidden-Message-Categories-for-SMS-and-MMS-in-the-US-and-Canada)

**U.K. phone numbers**

If any of your tenants have U.K. phone numbers, they will be unable to receive text messages sent from U.S. numbers. Due to policy changes implemented on June 1, 2023 by U.K. based mobile carries, text messages that originate from outside of the U.K. will be blocked. 

## Texting limit

There is a monthly limit on the number of messages you can send. You will be charged $.02 for every additional text message you send over that limit. Any overage charges you incur will be added to your next month's bill. See our [Terms of Service](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31742375286039-Mass-Text-Messaging) for more information.

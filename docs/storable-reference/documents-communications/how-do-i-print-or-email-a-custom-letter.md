# How do I print or email a custom letter?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18060446977687-How-do-I-print-or-email-a-custom-letter
**Article ID:** 18060446977687

---

Your custom templates can be viewed by hovering over the **Email, Txt & Print tab **and clicking Templates. If you need to create a new custom template you can find instructions in our article:

![Capture156.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27bb042863319924dd16/Capture156.PNG)

To print or email a custom template to a customer:

1\. Hover over the **Email, Txt & Print tab **and click **Print/Email letters**. ****

2\. You can use the filter to select which types of customers you want to send the letter/email to or you can select individual customers using the checkboxes in the list of tenants. 

![Capture151.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27bc042863319924dd18/Capture151.PNG)

3\. Select the Notification Template you would like to use. 

![Screenshot 2023-10-05 at 3.29.34 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/18060430573463)

4\. Select Email or Print as the _Contact Method_. 

![Capture158.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27bc042863319924dd19/Capture158.PNG)

5\. Click either Generate PDF or Send Emails.

## Printing a letter from the Customer Profile

From the Customer's Profile, click the **Letters** button. ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/6037ec99661b720174a70f43/file-wehrB0gdCB.png)3\. Select the template letter that you want to send to the customer from the N**otification template** dropdown. You can choose to print or email the template letter. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/6037ed5d8502d1120e90b47f/file-TyPuuM5Tl3.png)

(Pro Tip: The software will automatically fill in any [Placeholders](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18031379586071-Using-placeholders) to personalize the message once you send the letter, but if you want to preview what an email says before sending, choose the print option and generate the PDF to see the letter first before you go back and send as an email.)

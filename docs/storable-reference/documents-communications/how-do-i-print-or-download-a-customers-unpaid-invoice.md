# How do I print or download a customer's unpaid invoice?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15486142993687-How-do-I-print-or-download-a-customer-s-unpaid-invoice
**Article ID:** 15486142993687

---

Follow the instructions below to create and print an invoice for an individual customer.**  
**1\. Navigate to the customer's profile.

2\. Click**Make a Payment**. 

3\. Click **Download Invoice** in the top right corner of the screen. A PDF version of the invoice will be generated which can then be printed or saved to your computer. The PDF will include all currently, unpaid invoices on the account.

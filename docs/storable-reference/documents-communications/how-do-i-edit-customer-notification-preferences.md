# How do I edit customer notification preferences?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18059036858007-How-do-I-edit-customer-notification-preferences
**Article ID:** 18059036858007

---

If a customer wants to change which notices they receive, or the method of contact, you can adjust their settings by following the steps below.

1\. Go to your customer's profile. 

![4.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f2768042863319924dca8/4.JPG)

2\. In the Customer Information section, click the **Notification Preferences** button.

![5.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27682c7d3a272c0e0e08/5.png)

3\. The pop up will show you the current setting that a customer has selected on their account. You can then click **Edit Notification Preferences** to customize what notifications (and what type of notifications) your customer receives.

* Note, if you are not able to select a notification or contact method because the section is grayed out, that is because you haven't [turned on that notification](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18032140758423-Enabling-automatic-templates). 

![6.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f2769042863319924dca9/6.png)

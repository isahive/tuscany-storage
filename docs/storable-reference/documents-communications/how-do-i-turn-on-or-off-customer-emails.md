# How do I turn on or off customer emails?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/16793941386007-How-do-I-turn-on-or-off-customer-emails
**Article ID:** 16793941386007

---

Customers will receive email and text notifications based on how the facility has configured their templates under Email, Txt, & Print - Templates.

  
Follow the steps below to configure these settings at the individual customer level:

1\. Log in to your Storable Easy software.

2\. Click on the **Customers** tab.

![Capture120.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d502c7d3a272c0e1030/Capture120.png)

3\. Locate the customer you're wanting to change this option for. Click on their name to pull up their account.

4\. Click the **Notification Preferences** button. You will see all of your options for emailing, texting, and printing. In order to change them, click the **Edit Notification Preferences** text at the top of this pop-up box.

![Capture124.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d512c7d3a272c0e1032/Capture124.png)

![Capture126.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d51042863319924def1/Capture126.png)

If the template says _Not Enabled_ and a check box does not appear in this section then you will need to follow the steps below:

1\. Hover over the **Email, Txt, & Print** tab.

2\. Click the **Templates** sub-tab.

![Capture128.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d52042863319924def2/Capture128.png)

3\. Click on the template that is _Not Enabled_.

4\. Scroll to _Email Options_ or _Text Message Options_ and make sure that the desired method is enabled.

![Capture130.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d52042863319924def3/Capture130.png)

5) Click **Save.**

# How do I view or print a storage agreement?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15481771360791-How-do-I-view-or-print-a-storage-agreement
**Article ID:** 15481771360791

---

Follow the instructions below to view or print a customer's storage agreement.

## View a storage agreement for a current rental

1\. Go to the customer's profile by searching under the _Customers_ tab and clicking on the customer's name.

2\. In the _Rentals_ section of the customer's profile, click **View** next to _Storage Agreement_.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c65e5c62c7d3a66e32e7c60/file-Uix0uG0mBJ.png)

3\. This will bring you to the unit agreement details page. You can Email or Text the agreement by using the buttons in the upper right corner. To preview or print the document, click the blue link that says "Unit XXX Storage Agreement" next to _Pending Documents_ (unsigned agreements) or _Document_ (signed agreement). ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c5a1c1a2c7d3a66e32e0cf8/file-Fv5N6w0IWA.png)

4\. You can print the PDF using the **Print** button or save a copy to your computer.

You can also view all unsigned and signed storage agreements from the Storage Agreement report under Reports > Customers > Storage Agreements. Agreements can be downloaded from this report and links to the unsigned agreements can be sent to customers from the report when sorted by "Unsigned".

## View a storage agreement for a past rental

From the tenant's profile, click **Past Rentals** under the _Rentals_ section.

## ![past rentals.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31783732917271)

You'll see the Storage Agreement in the _Documents_ section.

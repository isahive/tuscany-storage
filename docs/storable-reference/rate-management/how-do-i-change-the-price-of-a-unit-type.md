# How do I change the price of a unit type?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15115877748759-How-do-I-change-the-price-of-a-unit-type
**Article ID:** 15115877748759

---

_**Note:**_ Changing the price of a unit type will NOT change the price of existing customers who are renting that size of unit. The new unit price will apply to new tenants only.

Changing the unit type price is the way to change your _Street Rate_. Follow the instructions below to change the Street Rate of a unit type.

## How to change the price of a unit type (unit size category)

1\. Log in to your software.

2\. Hover over the **Unit** tab and select **Unit Types**.

![1.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d492c7d3a272c0e1021/1.JPG)

3\. Locate the unit size of which you want to change the price. Click the far right **Edit** button on the line of that unit type.![2.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d492c7d3a272c0e1022/2.png)4\. Scroll down to the bottom where you will find a **price** field. Change the price to whatever you intend to rent the units for in the future.

![3.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d492c7d3a272c0e1023/3.png)

5\. Click **Update Unit Type**.

# Changing rent for a single tenant

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15693887456663-Changing-rent-for-a-single-tenant
**Article ID:** 15693887456663

---

Follow the instructions below to raise or lower the rental rate of a single current customer.

If you need to change rates for all tenants or for a Unit Type, you can find instructions in the articles below:

  * [How to mass edit rental prices](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/20064249790615-Mass-Edit-Rental-Prices)
  * [How to change the rental rate of a Unit Type](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15115877748759-How-do-I-change-the-price-of-a-unit-type)



1\. Navigate to the customer's profile. 

2\. Scroll down to the section of the page that says _Rentals_.

3\. Under the _Billing_ section, click **Edit**. ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/6532d899234cd75251327fc6/file-XUVtyyq8KQ.png)

4\. Enter the new rental rate in the **Monthly Price before Discount** field. You may need to choose _Custom_ from the _Plan_ dropdown menu in order to do this.

5\. Click **Update Rental** when you are done making changes.

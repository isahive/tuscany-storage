# Tenant Protection Plan: How do I add a protection plan to a tenant's account?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13820627039511-Tenant-Protection-Plan-How-do-I-add-a-protection-plan-to-a-tenant-s-account
**Article ID:** 13820627039511

---

Use the following steps to add a Tenant Protection Plan to a tenant's account:

1\. On the tenant's account under the _Rentals_ section, click the blue link **None** next to Tenant Protection.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5caba8682c7d3a154461e6ee/file-uGNeD3TItq.png)

2\. Select the desired plan from the drop down options, once a plan is selected click **Add plan**. (The prorated amount of the Tenant Protection Plan will display on the right) 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cabab862c7d3a154461e715/file-gpF5td7njy.png)

_Please note:_ Tax will be charged on the Tenant Protection Plan fee **only if** you've set up tax to be charged for rent.

# How do I change my facility contact information?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13821865892631-How-do-I-change-my-facility-contact-information
**Article ID:** 13821865892631

---

If you need to change your facility address, billing address, routing/account numbers, or phone number, follow the instructions below.

1\. Sign in to your Storable Easy software.

2\. Hover over the **Setup** tab and select **Contact**.

![1.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d562c7d3a272c0e1039/1.JPG)

3\. **General** \- This is where you can update the Facility Name and Website information. [[FACILITY_NAME]] and [[FACILITY_URL]] are the placeholders for this field. 

The **physical address** shows in the footer section of your website. It is also the address from which the Google Map pulls.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/6533005f9362491a4094acb8/file-9kOVGvamKW.png)

The **phone number** will show up on invoices and other templates when the [[facility_phone_number]] placeholder is used.

The **Billing Address** will show in invoices and other templates as your mailing address (using the [[facility_address]] placeholder) for checks and other payments.

# How do I change whether customers can change their own billing information?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14508690226583-How-do-I-change-whether-customers-can-change-their-own-billing-information
**Article ID:** 14508690226583

---

You can choose to allow or not allow customers to edit their billing information. If you would like to adjust this setting, follow the steps below:

1\. Go to the **Setup** tab.

2\. Click the **Settings** sub tab, scroll down, check or un-check the **Customers Can Edit Billing Information** box.

3\. Click **Save** at the bottom of the page to save any changes.

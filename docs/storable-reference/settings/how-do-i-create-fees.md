# How do I create fees?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14507691434775-How-do-I-create-fees
**Article ID:** 14507691434775

---

Follow the steps below to add fees into the _Add a Fee_ drop down menu that can be used to [charge tenants fees.](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14500439732119-How-do-I-manually-charge-a-tenant-a-fee-)

1\. Log into your account.

2\. Click the **Setup** tab.

3\. Click the **Fees** sub-tab.

![Capture24.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27dc042863319924dd4b/Capture24.PNG)

4\. Click **New Fee** at the top right corner.![Capture25.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27dc042863319924dd4c/Capture25.PNG)

5\. Fill in the fee information.

![Capture26.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27dc2c7d3a272c0e0e93/Capture26.PNG)

6\. Click **Save.**

# Adding new renter instructions

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14497705972503-Adding-new-renter-instructions
**Article ID:** 14497705972503

---

New Renter Instructions can be provided to tenants from the completed move-in pop up message and Rental Instructions email/text message. Learn how to edit these instructions below. 

## Completed move-in pop up

Tenants will see a pop up alert in their web browser once the move-in is complete. You can enter these instructions by navigating to Setup > Settings in the upper right hand corner.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bef0d4b04286304a71c5a2a/file-SUeQYj0tCX.png)Whatever you enter in the _New Renter_ Instructions box will be displayed to ALL customers once they complete their move-in. Be sure not to include any information that you don't want shared with every new customer.  
  
Click **Update Settings** when you are done.

## Rental Instructions email and text message

There is also an email that can be sent to new tenants. You can enter these instructions by navigating to TXT & Print > Templates

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c5b74c42c7d3a66e32e1ab9/file-eQZJJb8Tg7.png)

If the Email and Text boxes are marked with a green check, all customers with active emails and cell phone numbers will receive this notification. If you want to customize the alert, click on the template title. Click **Update Template** at the bottom once done.

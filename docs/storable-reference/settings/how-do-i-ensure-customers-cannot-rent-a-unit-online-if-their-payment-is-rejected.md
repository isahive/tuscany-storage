# How do I ensure customers cannot rent a unit online if their payment is rejected?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14508183168023-How-do-I-ensure-customers-cannot-rent-a-unit-online-if-their-payment-is-rejected
**Article ID:** 14508183168023

---

Follow the instructions below to require a successful payment for online rentals: 

1\. Log in to your software.

2\. Hover over the **Setup** tab and select **Settings**.

![1.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27782c7d3a272c0e0e19/1.JPG)

3\. Scroll down to the **Other** section, and uncheck the **Save Unpaid Customer Rentals**. 

![3.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27792c7d3a272c0e0e1a/3.JPG)

4\. Be sure to click the **Update Settings** button when you are done.

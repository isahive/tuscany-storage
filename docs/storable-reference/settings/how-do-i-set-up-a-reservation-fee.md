# How do I set up a reservation fee?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17630735534743-How-do-I-set-up-a-reservation-fee
**Article ID:** 17630735534743

---

You can setup a reservation fee in the software so that the system charges the customer a one time reservation fee when they reserve a unit online or in the office. Their fee will be applied towards the customer's first months rent, setup fee, and deposit once the reservation has been completed and turned into a rental.

## Add a reservation fee to a Unit

1\. Under the **Units** drop down, select **Unit Types**.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a66135c0428632faf6208b0/file-SjewEt3FO9.jpg)

2\. Click **Edit** to the far right of the desired unit type (if you want all units to have a reservation fee, you will need to complete this process separately for each unit type).

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6613af2c7d3a39e6263a3b/file-rxIa7r3h2q.png)

3\. In the **Reservation Price** section, input your desired reservation amount then click **Update** at the bottom. This amount will be applied to the customer's first months rent and deposit once the rental is completed. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6613d22c7d3a39e6263a3d/file-uZaW1ioTGR.png)

4\. On your website, you will now see a price associated with the reservation.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6613f22c7d3a39e6263a40/file-9u95fYcpfN.png)

When the tenant clicks to reserve, they will see a message that the reservation fee will be applied towards a rental deposit or invoice. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6614270428632faf6208ba/file-hg6ScNmXIQ.png)

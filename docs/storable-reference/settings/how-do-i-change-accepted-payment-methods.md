# How do I change accepted payment methods?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15341355272343-How-do-I-change-accepted-payment-methods
**Article ID:** 15341355272343

---

To change the accepted payment methods in your software, hover over the _Setup_ tab and click **Payment Processing**. 

![payment](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/15341324022039)

Select or deselect the desired accepted payment methods and update settings.

_**Please note the following when changing accepted payment methods:**_

  * Changing these settings will only apply to new payment accounts going forward. 
  * Disabling a payment method on this page will not disable active recurring billing accounts using that method. 
  * Tenants using a payment method you no longer accept will need to have their recurring payment account(s) deleted and they will need to provide a new payment account.

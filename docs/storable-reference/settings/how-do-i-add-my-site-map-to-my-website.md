# How do I add my Site Map to my Website?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14509065280535-How-do-I-add-my-Site-Map-to-my-Website
**Article ID:** 14509065280535

---

Follow the instructions below to display your Site Map on your Website:

1\. Hover over the  _Website_ tab, and click **Pages**.

![7.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f279e042863319924dceb/7.JPG)

2\. Click **Edit** next to the website tab which shows your rentals (usually _Rent A Unit_ or _Rent Storage_).

![8.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f279e2c7d3a272c0e0e46/8.png)

3\. Scroll down to the _Add a Widget_ section, and click the dropdown menu which says **Available Unit Types.**

![9.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f279e042863319924dcec/9.png)

4\. Under the menu, select **Site Map** , and click **Add**.

![10.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f279f042863319924dced/10.JPG)

7\. Click **Save** at the bottom of the new page.

![15.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f279f042863319924dcee/15.JPG)

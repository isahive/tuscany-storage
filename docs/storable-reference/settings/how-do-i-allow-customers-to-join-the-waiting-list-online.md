# How do I allow customers to join the Waiting List online?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18029174245655-How-do-I-allow-customers-to-join-the-Waiting-List-online
**Article ID:** 18029174245655

---

The waiting list is a feature where customers can sign up on a waiting list to rent a unit that is currently being rented by someone else or is simply unavailable at the moment. Customers can join the list online, or you can add them manually.

**1.** Hover over the **Website** tab and select **Pages**.

![3.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4c2c7d3a272c0ddc73/3.JPG)

**2.** Click on the **Edit** link next to the Rent Storage page.

![3.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4c2c7d3a272c0ddc74/3.png)

**3.** Scroll to the bottom and click **Edit** next to the Available Unit Types widget.

![4.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4d042863319924a98f/4.png)

**4.** Make sure the boxes are checked in the "Rent" and "Waiting List" columns. Click **Save.**

![5.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4d2c7d3a272c0ddc75/5.png)

**5.** Now, when a unit size is sold out, customers will see a _Waiting List_ button on your website.

![8.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4e2c7d3a272c0ddc76/8.JPG)

**6.** If a customer clicks on the _Waiting List_ button, they will be taken to a screen where they can input their information and be added to the waiting list report (see below).

![9.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4e2c7d3a272c0ddc77/9.JPG)

# Email notifications sent to software users

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18057297323159-Email-notifications-sent-to-software-users
**Article ID:** 18057297323159

---

The "Notifications Email" is the email that will receive all notifications from the software. 

## What email address will receive notifications from Storable Easy?

The email can be found or edited in your Easy software by hovering over the _Email, Txt & Print_ tab and clicking **Settings**.

![communication settings.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/18057332445335)

If you need to change the email address used for notifications, enter it in the **Notifications email** field. Multiple email addresses can be entered with a comma in between them.

![notifications email.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/18057291490839)

Click **Update Communication Settings** to save your changes.

## Emails sent to Managers/Owners from the software

The following emails can be sent automatically from Storable Easy to the _Notifications email._

  * When a credit without payment is added - sent only to the username marked as Owner
  * When a customer makes a payment - receipt 
  * When a customers payment has failed
  * Lockout and Lockout Removal report (once a day email)
  * Customers move out request (if enabled)
  * A new rental*, reservation or waiting list addition
  * When a customer adds a new Tenant Protection/Insurance plan
  * Rate management monthly reminder (if enabled)
  * Undelivered Notifications Report (once a day email)
  * Inbound text



You can customize which of these emails you receive from the _Email, Txt & Print_ tab > **Settings** page by checking or unchecking the boxes next to each email**.**

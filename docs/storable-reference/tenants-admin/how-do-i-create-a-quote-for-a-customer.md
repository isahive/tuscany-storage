# How do I create a quote for a customer?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17629530526103-How-do-I-create-a-quote-for-a-customer
**Article ID:** 17629530526103

---

If a customer would like a price for a unit they are thinking about renting, you can create a quote for them. You do not have to create a customer account in order to provide a quote.

1\. Under the **Customers** dropdown, select **New Quote**.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a60cbae2c7d3a67432f087f/file-II8RXri3Cy.jpg)

2\. Fill in the appropriate fields and click **Generate Quote** at the bottom.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a60d1c92c7d3a67432f08d0/file-gjR8KEw8iD.png)

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a60d1d30428635d7f439f87/file-7DRgGRg6Ho.png)

3\. On the new screen, you will see the total amount. You can also print or email the quote to the customer.

From this page, you can [create a new customer](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17720094554775-How-do-I-create-a-new-customer-account-) and finish renting the unit if the customer would like to move in.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a60d1e52c7d3a67432f08d2/file-4wQgjeAckf.png)

# Customer groups

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/32213618579351-Customer-groups
**Article ID:** 32213618579351

---

Groups can be used to target specific customer types when sending [email](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18058822026647-How-do-I-send-a-one-off-custom-email-to-customers) or [mass text messages](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31742375286039-Mass-Text-Messaging). You can also sort the _Customers_ page by groups.

The following groups exist within your Storable Easy software:

  * **Active:** All tenants - both current and past - who rented with you at one point.
  * **Current:** Tenants who are actively renting from you.
  * **Recurring:** Tenants set up with recurring payments (e.g., autopay).
  * **Non-Recurring:** Tenants not enrolled in autopay; they pay manually each billing cycle.
  * **Outstanding:** Tenants with an account that has a balance due — including both pending and past due invoices.
  * **Past Due:** Tenants with an account that has a past due balance.
  * **Late:** Tenants who have met your criteria of days past due resulting in a late label.
  * **Locked Out:** Tenants who are currently locked out of their unit due to nonpayment or another account issue.
  * **Auction Date Set:** Accounts with a scheduled auction due to nonpayment.
  * **Waiting List:** Potential tenants who’ve signed up for notifications about available units.
  * **Late Fee Exempt:** Current tenants who don’t incur late fees, likely due to special status or arrangement.
  * **Tax Exempt:** Tenants whose rental charges are exempt from taxes (e.g., nonprofits or qualifying government entities).
  * **Active Tenant Protection Plans:** Tenants currently enrolled in a tenant protection plan.
  * **Active Insurance Policies:** Tenants currently enrolled in an insurance policy.
  * **Tenant Protection Eligible:** Tenants who are eligible to enroll in a protection plan but haven’t done so yet.
  * **Automatic Lockout Disabled:** Tenants whose accounts have been flagged to prevent automated lockouts.

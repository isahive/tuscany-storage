# Finalizing or canceling a scheduled move-out

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15480978718487-Finalizing-or-canceling-a-scheduled-move-out
**Article ID:** 15480978718487

---

**Note:**  _The system will NOT automatically move someone out on the scheduled move out date. You must do that manually._

Follow the instruction below to finalize or cancel a scheduled move-out. You can read more about move-out request settings in our article: [Customer scheduled move-outs.](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15454090162327-Customer-scheduled-move-outs)

1\. Log in to your Storable Easy account.

2\. Navigate to the _Customer_ page for the tenant who has a scheduled move-out. 

**![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a21eb78042863740565403c/file-Ndse6xVWoi.png)**

3**.** From the _Rentals_ section of the page, you can finalize, cancel, or edit the scheduled move-out.

  * The **Edit** button will allow you to change the date of the move-out.
  * The **Finalize****Move Out** button will allow you to finalize the move-out right now. 
  * The **Cancel** button will cancel the scheduled move-out. 



![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a21ec9a2c7d3a71c72bebaf/file-ZeDICWVF32.png)

When moving a customer out, we recommend the following:

  * Pause or Delete the Recurring Billing Payment Account (To avoid charges on a customer account, if invoices were not voided).
  * Void any outstanding invoices (To avoid reports showing outstanding balances. If the move-out is finalized before the current invoice due date the software will automatically void the invoice.)
  * Archive the customer's profile (Edit Profile>Archive Customer, archiving is an option also available during the move-out process).

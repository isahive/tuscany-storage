# How do I view the Waiting List?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18029213479063-How-do-I-view-the-Waiting-List
**Article ID:** 18029213479063

---

**1.** To see who is on the Waiting List, or to [add someone manually to the list](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18028976404759-How-do-I-add-someone-to-the-Waiting-List-), go to the Report tab and click **All Reports**.

![Reports_Drop_Down.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4e042863319924a990/Reports_Drop_Down.JPG)

**2.** Under the Customer's column, select **Waiting List**.

![1.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af4e2c7d3a272c0ddc78/1.png)

**3.** Here, you can see who is on the Waiting List as well as who is scheduled to move out. The Waiting List will show how many units are available of the desired unit type, so you can rent from the list. It can also be sorted or downloaded as seen below. Customers will NOT be notified when a unit has become available. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bc79c60042863158cc78efc/file-RNdcJD69kd.png)

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bc79c592c7d3a04dd5bcfa1/file-FTBWCEaM1Y.jpg)

_NOTE:_ When a customer is on the Waiting List and is not currently renting another unit at the facility, removing them from the Waiting List will archive their customer account.

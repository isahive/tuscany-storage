# How do I charge a customer for a retail item?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17629203013655-How-do-I-charge-a-customer-for-a-retail-item
**Article ID:** 17629203013655

---

Follow the instructions below to charge a customer for a retail item they are purchasing. Note: The item must first be [created in the Retail Sale](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17628024737687-Retail-Sale) area of your software. 

1\. Locate the Customer's account from Customers > All Customers or use the Search Customers bar.

2\. Click the tenant's name to go into their profile. 

3\. Click the _Fees/Products_ tab.

![fees products.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17629203005591)

4\. Select the product they are purchasing from the **Add a product** dropdown menu. 

![Capture95.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d772c7d3a272c0e1079/Capture95.PNG)

5\. Confirm the details of the retail item and click **Add product**. 

![Screenshot 2023-09-18 at 3.09.58 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17629216906135)

6\. Click **Save**. 

![add retail item.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17629223892759)

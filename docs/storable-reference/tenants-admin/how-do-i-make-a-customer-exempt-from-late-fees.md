# How do I make a customer exempt from Late fees?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/20870529495575-How-do-I-make-a-customer-exempt-from-Late-fees
**Article ID:** 20870529495575

---

As a general rule, the software will automatically apply Late and Lien Fees to all customers who do not pay their bill and reach the number of _Days Past Due_ indicated under [Setup > Late/Lien](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14501720951191-Late-Lien-settings).

  
If you do not want to charge late fees for a specific tenant, you can mark their profile as _Late Fee Exempt_ and prevent automatic fees from being invoiced on their account.

1\. Use the[ _List View_](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14871969658647-Unit-List-View) or [ _Customer Search_](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15005022431255-Accessing-customer-accounts) to locate and open the Tenant Profile.

2\. At the top of the profile, click **Edit Profile.**

![edit profile.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/20870529489559)

3\. At the bottom of the page, under _Notes_ , check the **Late Fee Exempt?** box.

![late fee exempt.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/20870530853143)

4\. Click **Update Customer** at the bottom of the page to save your change.

If you choose to make a tenant exempt from late fees, the tenant will not be charged the late fee or any other fees that are setup in your [Late/Lien](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14501720951191-Late-Lien-settings) settings. Depending on the situation for the exemption, it is advisable that you also [disable the customer's Late/Lien notifications](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15456761911831-How-do-I-enable-or-disable-late-notices-for-a-customer).

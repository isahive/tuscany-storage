# How do I archive customers?

**Article ID:** 17628650406295

## Archive Customer Account

After a customer moves out, you may want to archive their customer account. Follow the instructions below to archive the account. 

1\. Locate Customer account from  _Customers > All Customers_ OR use the Search Customers bar.

2\. Click on the customer’s name.

3\. Click **Edit Profile** _._  
![edit profile.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17628636396439)

![](blob:https://sparefoot.atlassian.net/55bf329c-e1fc-48ce-93cc-e143ba3640a8#media-blob-url=true&id=8a2ee0be-e2a6-4794-aa09-ee980f04810e&collection=contentId-3836543014&contextId=3836543014&height=438&width=749&alt=)

4\. Click **Archive**.  
  
![archive.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17628609020823)

![](blob:https://sparefoot.atlassian.net/74e3bfb2-5550-405a-bd13-a430ef850dd0#media-blob-url=true&id=8a2ee0be-e2a6-4794-aa09-ee980f04810e&collection=contentId-3836543014&contextId=3836543014&height=398&width=791&alt=)

Note: Customer Accounts cannot be archived if they are renting a unit.  
Customer accounts can NOT be deleted.   
Customers can NOT login once their account has been archived. 

## Locating an archived customer account

1\. Hover over the **Customers** tab.

2\. Click the **All Customers.**

3\. Type in the customers name.

4\. Select **Archived** from the status drop down menu.

![archived.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17628624439703)

5\. Click **Search.**

## Unarchive a customer

1\. Find the archived customer by following the instructions above.

2\. Click their name.

3\. Click **Un-Archive.**

# How do I enable or disable late notices for a customer?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15456761911831-How-do-I-enable-or-disable-late-notices-for-a-customer
**Article ID:** 15456761911831

---

To disable/enable notifications for a customer, follow these steps:

1\. Go to the customer's profile.

2\. Click **Notification Preferences** (located in the bottom right of the Customers Information section).

![notification](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/15456924874263)

3\. Click **Edit** to the right of _Notification Preferences_.

![edit](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/15456924875159)

4\. Check or uncheck the boxes for email and text for each notification type.

  * Automatic notifications are available to disable/enable only if the automatic notification template is enabled.
  * Late/Lien notifications are available to disable/enable only for Late/Lien rules that have an email, text, or letter enabled.



5\. Click **Update Notification** Preferences.

![update](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/15456882650263)

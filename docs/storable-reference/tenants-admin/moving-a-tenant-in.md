# Moving a tenant in

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15694613316119-Moving-a-tenant-in
**Article ID:** 15694613316119

---

You can rent a unit to a customer from either the **Units** tab or the **Customers** tab.

If you are in the process of entering your tenant data for the first time, we recommend using the Units Tab because it is faster for multiple rentals. 

## From the Units Tab:

1**.** Hover over the _Units_ tab and select **List View**. ![1.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27af042863319924dd01/1.JPG) | 

## From the Customers Tab:

1\. Hover over the _Customer Tab_ and click **All Customers.** ![30.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b72c7d3a272c0e0e62/30.JPG)  
---|---  
2**.** Click the unit number from the list. If you will be renting multiple units, we recommend right clicking the unit number and opening it in a new tab, so you can leave this page open to rent the next unit. (If you do not see units listed, you need to create unit types and units before you can rent them to a customer.)  ![2.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27af042863319924dd02/2.png) |  2\. Find and select the customer you are renting the unit to.  ![32.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b72c7d3a272c0e0e63/32.png)  
3**.** Select the customer from the _New Customer_ dropdown menu. Then, click **Rent to Customer**. ![3.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b02c7d3a272c0e0e5a/3.png) |  3**.** Click **Rent a Unit** at the top of the page. ![33.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b8042863319924dd0e/33.png)  
4\. The unit type and number will already be selected on this screen. This is the difference between renting from the unit tab and from the customer page. In the customer page, the unit type and number will not be pre-selected on this screen. ![21.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b02c7d3a272c0e0e5c/21.JPG) |  4\. Select the _Unit Size_ and _Number._ ![34.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b8042863319924dd0f/34.png)  
  
The following steps will be applicable whether you started from the Units tab or the Customers tab.

5**.** Enter the start date for the customer's billing cycle. **The billing cycle start date must be a future date.** The date you put here will be the date your customer is due for rent and the software will invoice according to the due date and whatever you have the [billing period](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14150546685591-How-do-I-change-the-tenant-Billing-Period-) set as. If you want all tenants to be due on the first of the month, you would select the next first of a month as the date.

_![5.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b12c7d3a272c0e0e5d/5.JPG)_

_Please Note:_ If you are entering your tenants for the first time and will be using the software for the coming month, you will want to set the due date out to the next month or to the month when you plan to start using the software. If you fail to do this, the software will generate invoices that will then have to be voided out manually, one by one.

6\. The _Plan_ field is the price your customer will be paying monthly. It will automatically populate the price of the unit. 

![24.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b1042863319924dd03/24.JPG)

If you'd like to enter a different amount than shown, select **Custom** in the _Plan_ dropdown.

![6.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b1042863319924dd04/6.JPG)

**Monthly Price before Discount:** If the customer is paying a different monthly price than the current unit price, enter their rate in the _Monthly Price before Discount_ field. Leave the _Discount_ at 0.0 and the _Billing Cycle_ at 1 month. This will not change the price of the unit for anyone else, only for this specific customer. 

**If you want to give the customer a percentage discount:** Input the full price of the unit in the _Monthly Price Before Discoun_ t field, and input the discount in the _Discount_ field. 

**If the customer wants to pay once a year or a few months at a time:** Select the number of months the tenant is paying for from the _Billing Cycle_ dropdown menu. For example, yearly payments would be a "12 month" billing cycle. Every six months would be a "6 month" billing cycle. 

**If you want to rent a unit to someone at no charge** , put any amount in the _Monthly Price before Discount_ field (it cannot be zero) and then give 100% discount. 

![22.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b22c7d3a272c0e0e5e/22.JPG)

7\. If the customer has an insurance plan, you can select it here. (Before options will be shown in this box, you must first add the insurance plans under Setup > Insurance > Add (button under available plans).

![7.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b3042863319924dd05/7.JPG)

8\. If you do not charge a deposit, make sure the box is unchecked. If you DO charge a deposit, check **Charge Deposit** and read the rest of this step. 

**Note:** The deposit amount can be refunded when a customer moves out. The setup fee (see step 10) is not typically refunded. **Tax is not charged on the deposit. If you charge tax, you need to reflect that in the deposit amount.**

![8.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b3042863319924dd06/8.JPG)

After checking _Charge Deposit_ , you will see the deposit amount and also another box giving you the option to mark the deposit as previously paid. If the customer paid their deposit in the past, check that box as well. 

![9.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b4042863319924dd07/9.png)

9\. If you do not charge a setup fee, make sure _Charge Setup Fee_ is not checked. If you DO charge a setup fee, check the box and continue reading.

![10.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b42c7d3a272c0e0e5f/10.JPG)

After checking the box, the setup fee and an option to charge tax will appear. (If you need to charge tax but don't see the option in the dropdown box, you have to first add tax options under the setup tab > Tax Rates > New Tax Rate.)

![11.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b4042863319924dd08/11.JPG)

10\. If the customer has an outstanding balance (owes you money), select **Balance Forward from** the _Add a fee_ dropdown.

![12.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b5042863319924dd09/12.JPG)

A new page will come up. You can change the name to whatever you'd like (Unpaid rent for June, etc.). Enter the amount the customer owes you. When done, click **Add a Fee**. When you finish the renting process, this amount will show outstanding on the customer's balance.

![13.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b5042863319924dd0a/13.JPG)

11\. You can select your preferred proration option from the _Pro-Rating Options_ dropdown box. The most commonly used are the last two listed.

![14.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b5042863319924dd0b/14.JPG)

12\. The move-in date can be any time in the past. If your customer moved in June 2014, simply type in the date in the box or select it from the calendar. 

![15.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b5042863319924dd0c/15.JPG)

13\. If you have a state tax, you can select it from the _Tax rate_ dropdown. If you need to charge tax but don't see the correct option in the dropdown box, you have to first add tax options under the Setup Tab > Tax Rates > New Tax Rate.

![16.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b6042863319924dd0d/16.JPG)

14\. Double check the prices and then click **Rent Now**. The deposit will show as a charge even if you clicked the _Mark as previously paid_ button. The system will mark it as paid after you click the _Rent Now_ button. 

![25.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b62c7d3a272c0e0e60/25.JPG)

15\. The _Rentals_ section of the customer's page will show all the information you input. If you marked the deposit as previously paid, it will show that here as well.

**![18.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27b62c7d3a272c0e0e61/18.JPG)**

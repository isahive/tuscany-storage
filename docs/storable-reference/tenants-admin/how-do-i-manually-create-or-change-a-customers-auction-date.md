# How do I manually create or change a customer's auction date?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15453815149847-How-do-I-manually-create-or-change-a-customer-s-auction-date
**Article ID:** 15453815149847

---

To create a custom auction date, first go to the customer's page.

2\. Click on the **Gate Access** button in the right corner.![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a21e0940428637405653ffa/file-ZAWi7VesZU.png)

3\. Click in the blank space under **Auction Date** , and select the date of the auction. You can also update the auction date here if you need to change their date of sale.![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/6532c56d234cd75251327faf/file-IDbhc1jKO6.png)

4\. Click the **Lock Out** button.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a21e0e62c7d3a71c72beb72/file-xK3clom6kN.png)

5\. Change the status to **Auction**. Click **Save**. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a21e0fa2c7d3a71c72beb73/file-qekGqLdkuf.jpg)

6\. The status has now changed to Auction. Note that this auction date will not show on the dashboard until the day of the auction. This date will show up in any templates that use the placeholder  _[[AUCTION_DATE]]_.![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a21e12e0428637405653ffb/file-tgLBbGwplM.png)

# How does my customer change their login information?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19153062151703-How-does-my-customer-change-their-login-information
**Article ID:** 19153062151703

---

1\. To change their login information, a customer will go to your website and click **Make a Payment/Login** and enter their login credentials. 

2\. They will click **Edit Profile** at the top of the screen. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/62b23ae30d593668f7e576cf/file-fn1Vy6lirH.png)

3\. From here, they will be able to edit their Email or Username at any time. They also have the option to get a reset password link sent to their email.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/653aeccb283d7851b4fe9cc2/file-fApstxP1uO.png)

If you want your customer to be able to edit/change their profile information i.e. address, phone, etc. you will turn on the setting under **Setup > Settings > Other**. Check the box next to Customer can edit profile information and save. This will allow your customer to edit any information on their account.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/62b23b67a713c738b2978a95/file-HHlFgz6OJQ.png)

  
Any changes made by a customer (or manager) will be tracked under the Alterations on the customer page (button at the bottom of the customer profile page) as well as the Alteration report.

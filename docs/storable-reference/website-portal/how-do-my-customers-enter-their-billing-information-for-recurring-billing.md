# How do my customers enter their billing information for recurring billing?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19130189097495-How-do-my-customers-enter-their-billing-information-for-recurring-billing
**Article ID:** 19130189097495

---

Customers can enter their billing information in their tenant portal by following the directions below. 

1\. Your customer needs to sign in through your website by clicking **Make a Payment/Login.**

2\. Direct them to the _Recurring Billing_ tab.

3\. They can add a payment account themselves by clicking **Add Payment Account**. They'll also be able to _Pause_ or _Edit_ any card on file already.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9a52020428633d2cf41031/file-Nd32l33aWF.png)Keep in mind, your customers will NOT be able to edit this information if the **Customers Can Edit Billing Information** box is unchecked under _Setup and Settings_. If you want them to be able to edit this, make sure this is checked!

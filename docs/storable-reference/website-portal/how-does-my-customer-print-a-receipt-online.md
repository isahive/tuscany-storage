# How does my customer print a receipt online?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19153111906711-How-does-my-customer-print-a-receipt-online
**Article ID:** 19153111906711

---

1\. After [signing in to their account](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17630459947159-How-does-a-customer-log-into-their-customer-portal-) from your website, the customer will click on the date to the left of whichever payment line item they want to print.  
![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9a54892c7d3a1544615bae/file-L7JeoUHt94.png)

2\. They will then click the **Print** button at the bottom.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9a551c0428633d2cf41068/file-C8JceoJIMr.png)

3\. On the new screen, they will click the **Print This Page** button in the top right.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9a555e2c7d3a1544615bc4/file-ZQ6oGjbnp1.png)

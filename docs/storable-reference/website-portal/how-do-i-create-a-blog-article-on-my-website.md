# How do I create a blog article on my website?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18082789513367-How-do-I-create-a-blog-article-on-my-website
**Article ID:** 18082789513367

---

Follow the instructions below to create a new blog article on your website. 

1\. The first thing you'll do is upload any pictures associated with your article. To do so, hover over the _Website_ tab and click **Image library.**

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cc07c2b2c7d3a026fd3ffa2/file-0KgTjCpS6P.png)

2\. Click **Add an Image** and upload any images you want to include in the article.

3\. Then, hover over the _Website_ tab and click **Blog**. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cc07d3004286301e753b287/file-4mkF3ZykYU.png)

4\. Click **New Blog Post** in the top right. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cc07d5e04286301e753b28d/file-yVzW4rKbfH.png)5\. Enter a title (of the article), slug (one word that will be added to the end of the url), and content of the article. To add pictures from your library, click the picture icon above the content box. You do not need to worry about entering Meta keywords or a Meta description.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cc07de204286301e753b299/file-R6oRrn64SJ.png)

6\. When you are done, click **Publish.**

## Add a list of recent blog posts to your website's home page

In order for blog posts to appear on the home page of your website, you will need to add the _List of Recent Blog Posts_ widget to your website home page.

1\. Hover over the _Website_ tab and click **Pages**. 

2\. Click **edit** for the _Home page_.

3\. Scroll to the bottom and add the _List of Recent Blog Posts_ widget.

4\. Click **Save.**

5\. The widget will default to the top of your home page widgets. Drag and drop the blog post widget to the bottom of the widget list and save.

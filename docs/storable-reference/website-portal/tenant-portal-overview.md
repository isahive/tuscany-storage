# Tenant portal overview

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19152848770583-Tenant-portal-overview
**Article ID:** 19152848770583

---

Once your clients have [logged in to their accounts](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17630459947159-How-does-a-customer-log-into-their-customer-portal-) through your website, they can:

1\. [Make a payment](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19152986697239-How-does-my-customer-make-a-payment-online-) (Dashboard)

2\. [Change their user name or password](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19153062151703-How-does-my-customer-change-their-login-information-) (Edit Profile)

3\. [See their billing history](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19153236790295-How-does-my-customer-print-an-account-history-) (Dashboard)

4\. View any unpaid balance (Dashboard)

5\. Change or add an email (Edit Profile)

6\. Print an invoice or account statement (Make a Payment)

7\. Rent or Reserve Another Unit

8\. [Schedule a Move-out](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15454090162327-Customer-scheduled-move-outs) (Optional)

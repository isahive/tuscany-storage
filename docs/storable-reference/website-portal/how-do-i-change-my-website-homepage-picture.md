# How do I change my website homepage picture?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18061194120471-How-do-I-change-my-website-homepage-picture
**Article ID:** 18061194120471

---

Follow the instructions below to change your website picture.

1\. Hover your mouse over the **Website** tab and click on the **Pages** sub-tab. 

![Capture178.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27d92c7d3a272c0e0e8f/Capture178.png)

2\. Click **edit** next to the _Home_ page. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5beefb4e2c7d3a31944e0de7/file-EerSIwGRP9.png)

3\. Scroll down and click **Edit** next to _Picture With Overlay Text_ widget. 

![Capture185.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27da042863319924dd46/Capture185.png)

4\. Under the current picture, check the check-box that says **Remove.** Then, click **Save** at the bottom.

5\. Click **edit** next to the Home page again and upload a new picture.

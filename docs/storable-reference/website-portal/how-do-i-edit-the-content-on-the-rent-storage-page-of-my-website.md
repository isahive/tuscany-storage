# How do I edit the content on the "Rent Storage" page of my website?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18082396001431-How-do-I-edit-the-content-on-the-Rent-Storage-page-of-my-website
**Article ID:** 18082396001431

---

Follow the instructions below if you would like to edit the verbiage on the _Rent Storage_ of your website.

1\. Log into your Storable Easy management software.

2\. Hover your mouse over the **WEBSITE** tab, then clicking on the **PAGES** sub-tab.

3\. Click **Edit** by "_Rent Storage_ ".

![rentstorage1.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27d8042863319924dd44/rentstorage1.png)

4\. Scroll down to where you see "_Available Unit Types_ " widget and click **E****dit** again. 

![rentstorage_2.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27d8042863319924dd45/rentstorage_2.png)

5\. Check the boxes next to available [unit types](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13791187010327-How-do-I-create-a-Unit-Type-) that you would like to appear on your website page as available to rent, reserve, or join the waiting list. Click **Update Widget** when you are done making changes.

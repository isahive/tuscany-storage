# How do I change the colors on my website?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18090220685079-How-do-I-change-the-colors-on-my-website
**Article ID:** 18090220685079

---

Follow the instructions below to customize the colors on your website.

  1. Log into your Storable Easy software.
  2. Hover over the **Website** tab and click **Settings**.
  3. Under _Theme Settings,_ you will see **Color Customization.**



![Screenshot 2023-10-06 at 3.46.26 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/18090192127639)

4\. There are multiple themes built into the software or you can change the individual colors for each section. Click the color/s you want to change to choose a new one.

![Screenshot 2023-10-06 at 3.47.56 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/18090210257431)

Clicking **Reset Colors** will put them back to the colors they were the last time the _Update Settings_ button was pressed

5\. Don't forget to hit **Update Settings** at the bottom of the page when you are done making changes.

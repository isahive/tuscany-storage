# How do I enable online reservations?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17630001757719-How-do-I-enable-online-reservations
**Article ID:** 17630001757719

---

You can allow customers to make reservations on your website by following the instructions below. **Note: You must integrate your Storable Easy software with a payment provider for customers to rent online.** If you have not signed up for Easy Payments or Auth.net/Moneris (Canadian facilities only), the "Rent Unit" button will not show up online even if you follow these steps.

## Enable Online Reservations

1\. Under the **Website** dropdown, select **Pages**.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6611bd2c7d3a39e6263a14/file-BgKdXmcrqP.jpg)

2\. On the  _Rent Storage_ page, click **Edit**.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6611e72c7d3a39e6263a1a/file-ZYxpeN8SnY.png)

3\. Scroll down and click **Edit** next to  _Available unit types._

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6612022c7d3a39e6263a1c/file-g4kHozKafI.png)

4\. Choose what you would want customers to be able to do online (rent, reserve, be added to a waiting list if that sized unit is not available, or tell them to contact you).![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bec73ea2c7d3a31944dfaff/file-2iylbkp5mZ.png)

5\. Click **Update** at the bottom of the page.

6\. On your website, you will now see a Reserve button on the rental page.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a6612ad2c7d3a39e6263a27/file-gih4UBiF5m.png)

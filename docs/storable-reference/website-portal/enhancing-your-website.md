# Enhancing your website

---

Below are a few recommendations to enhance your Storable Easy website and make it look more custom and unique to your facility. Understanding HTML code and working with widgets will be very helpful. Google is a great reference for self-teaching.

## Add a Google Review button

Follow the directions below to add a button that will allow customers to leave a Google review of your facility. 

*Note: You will need a Google My Business account set up to do this. If you don’t have a Google My Business account, click [HERE](https://www.google.com/business/?ppsrc=GPDA2) to begin that process. 

1\. In your Storable Easy software, hover over the _Website_ dropdown menu and click **Pages**. | ![pages.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931539052183)  
---|---  
2\. Click **Edit** next to the _Home_ page.  | ![edit home.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931549386903)  
3\. Click **Edit** next to the _Picture with overlay_ _text_ widget. | ![edit picture with overlay.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931549390103)  
4\. Click the HTML icon in the menu at the top of the Content Box. | ![html.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931549392791)  
5\. Add the following HTML code below to the Content box. Replace the GOOGLE REVIEW LINK text in the code with your Google Review link. To find your Google My Business link, go to your Google My Business Page. Click **Reviews** and then click **Write a Review**. Copy the URL.  
      
    
    <div style="margin:0 auto;">  
      
    <p style="text-align:;"><a href="GOOGLE REVIEW LINK" class="btn btn-lg btn-secondary">Leave A Review</a></p>  
      
    </div>

6\. Click **Update Widget.**

The widget on your website will look similar to the example below:

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26928519787671)

## Adding a Google Review Widget

Follow the directions below to add a link on your website to your Google reviews.

Find an image of the Google logo. To do this, you can search “Google” and select images. Screenshot a Google Logo and add it to your Storable Easy [Image Library](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18078801133079-Website-images).

1\. In your Easy software, hover over the _Website_ dropdown menu and click **Pages**. | ![pages.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931539052183)  
---|---  
2\. Click **Edit** next to the page you want to add the widget to. | ![edit home.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931549386903)  
3\. Select **One column** from the _Add a Widget_ dropdown menu. Click **Add**. ![Screenshot 2024-10-08 at 2.59.53 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931549394711)  
4\. Add a _Widget Title_ such as "L Street Storage Reviews" using the name of your facility.  
5\. In the Content box. enter “Click below to see what our customers have to say!”. Format the content. We recommend selecting H2 for the font size and centering the text.  | ![h2.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/26931549396631)  
6\. Add the Google Logo below the text and center it.   
7\. Make the logo a button by highlighting the logo and clicking on the link icon to add your Google Reviews link. To find your Google Reviews link, go to your Google My Business Page. Click **Reviews.** Copy the URL.  
8\. Click **Save**.

# How does my customer print an account history?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19153236790295-How-does-my-customer-print-an-account-history
**Article ID:** 19153236790295

---

1\. First, your customer will need to [sign in to your website](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17630459947159-How-does-a-customer-log-into-their-customer-portal-).

2\. Their page will look like the one shown here.  
![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9a53110428633d2cf41043/file-WzUyefNRTI.png)

3\. Have them scroll all the way to the bottom of the page and click **Full History**.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9a53350428633d2cf41045/file-Y59wnJpjMv.png)

4\. From here, they will need to scroll all the way to the bottom again and click **Download as PDF**.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9a536a0428633d2cf4104b/file-3RpC09E884.png)

5\. The history will open in a new screen as a PDF. (If the customer is using Explorer, the pdf will likely download to the bottom of their screen). If the customer hovers their mouse over the top, a bar will appear from which they can download the document to his computer or print it.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a21e6bc0428637405654027/file-dBLVm3GCJY.jpg)

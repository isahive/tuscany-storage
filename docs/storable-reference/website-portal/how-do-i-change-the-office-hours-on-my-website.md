# How do I change the office hours on my website?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18089192134551-How-do-I-change-the-office-hours-on-my-website
**Article ID:** 18089192134551

---

Follow the instructions below to change the office hours of your facility that are shown on your website.

Hover over the **Website** tab and click **Settings**.

Scroll down to the bottom of the page to the Office Hours section. You can change whether your office is open or closed and and the hours it is open for each day of the week. You can also change the name of the hours (e.g. Facility Hours).

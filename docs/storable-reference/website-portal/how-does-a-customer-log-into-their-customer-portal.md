# How does a customer log into their customer portal?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17630459947159-How-does-a-customer-log-into-their-customer-portal
**Article ID:** 17630459947159

---

Your customer can log in directly on your website by using the steps below.

1\. On your website, click **Make a Payment/Login**.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27d2042863319924dd39/mceclip0.png)

2\. Enter login information. The customer will use the login information that [you created for them](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17720204367639) when you set them up as a customer. After the first time they log in, they can change both their username and password if they would like to.

_**Please note:**_ Usernames are case sensitive.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27d2042863319924dd3a/mceclip1.png)

3\. Click **Log In.**

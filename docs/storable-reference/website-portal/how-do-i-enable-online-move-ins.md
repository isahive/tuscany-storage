# How do I enable online move-ins?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17631129755159-How-do-I-enable-online-move-ins
**Article ID:** 17631129755159

---

**Note: If you have not signed up with a credit card processing company, the "Rent Unit" button will not show up online even if you follow these steps.**

1\. Navigate to your Website Tab and click **Pages**.

2\. Click **edit** next to Rent Storage. (This might say Rent Storage Units/ your city and state).![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bec73e104286304a71c46ca/file-rf6I67Z3EU.png)

3\. Scroll to the bottom of the page and under Current Widgets, click **Edit** under _Available unit types_.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bec73e62c7d3a31944dfafe/file-FNpFbOAjPz.png)

4\. Check the boxes for what you want customer to be able to do online. ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bec73ea2c7d3a31944dfaff/file-2iylbkp5mZ.png)

5\. Click **Update Widget.**

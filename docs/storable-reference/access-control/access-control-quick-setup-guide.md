# Access Control: Quick Setup Guide

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14014734450071-Access-Control-Quick-Setup-Guide
**Article ID:** 14014734450071

---

**1\. Hang Cloud Controller**

Mount Controller in a climatized unit or office (Min-Max operating temps are -4 to 140 degrees F).

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5d9deaed04286364bc902f57/file-Azj3I5rVRN.png)

There are 3 mounting holes in the case of the Cloud Controller.

![step1and5.jpg](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/38361129314711)  
**2\. Run Wire**

Run 22/6 Shielded wire to each keypad from the cloud controller.

Run 18/2 stranded wire to the gate operator motor from the cloud controller (This is not the gate power, the gate operator will need its own power supply).

**NOTE:** Ethernet cable or Cat 5/6 is not recommended for a keypad or operator wiring due to smaller gauge wire and possible electrical interference. [Learn more.](https://support.pdk.io/hc/en-us/articles/360046225194-FAQ-DO-NOT-use-CAT5-CAT5E-CAT6-and-CAT6E-for-Reader-Wiring)

![step2.jpg](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/38361206502551)  


**3\. Mount and Wire Keypad(s)**

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/62f139ff61ff5d5f24f99d5b/file-arF3AXcmYT.png)

a. Remove the keypad mounting bracket. Slide keypad wiring through the keypad bracket, buffer plate center hole and connect keypad wiring with B-connector splices (RED, BLACK, GREEN, WHITE, BROWN). *It is possible to pull wiring through after mounting but only if the splices used will fit through the hole.*

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/62f139d95c214850abad4594/file-aF8WI8AjqY.png)

b. Mount keypad buffer plate to face of gooseneck pedestal with carriage bolts (not included).

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5fbbe5ae4cedfd001610f9bd/file-SPALgyRCcN.png)

c. Slide wiring back into the pedestal, line up, and secure the keypad mounting bracket into the two guide holes on the buffer plate face.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5fbbe696cff47e00160bcb26/file-Wm1rQdksbf.jpg)

d. Re-mount the Push Button Keypad reader onto to keypad bracket and tighten the bracket screws.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5fbbeb4e4cedfd001610f9ee/file-gjzQ04AwSp.png)

**4\. Wire Gate Operator Motor**

**![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5e1f970e04286364bc93f748/file-ktRYMkBXyW.png)**  


Check your gate opener motor manual for correct terminals to connect 18/2 wire. [Learn more.](https://support.pdk.io/hc/en-us/articles/8456372680215-FAQ-How-to-connect-a-Red-series-Controller-to-a-commercial-gate-operator-using-an-isolation-relay)

**5\. Wire Cloud Controller**

Wire Keypads as pictured Below (22/6 wire)

Gate operators wire to C and NO (18/2 wire)

Connect positive and common to the power supply terminals.

![step1and5.jpg](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/38361129314711)

Wire Power Supply.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5cdef66c0428634b85595e93/file-WPe858wdHm.jpg)

Plug in Ethernet.

Check for Ethernet activity lights. See Image below. 

(You should see a flashing green on the right and a solid orange on the left.)

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a282a4a0428631b6b6db341/file-rRmBukdkd5.jpg)**6\. Power up System**

Plug the Transformer into an electrical outlet.

**NOTE:** Do not disconnect power or ethernet for at least 5 minutes after powering up the cloud controller. When it first turns on it checks for updates and downloads/installs the latest version.

**7\. Call us at 435-656-1990 Option 2 for Configuration and Website Integration.**

Once everything is wired and connected call us to configure your controller(s) and integrate your Storable Easy website.

**(Optional) Connecting a 12Volt Electric Door Strike to a cloud controller or single door controller.**

  * Power the strike from the positive terminal on the first grey connector and connect the negative side to NO.
  * Use the supplied Diode between positive and common from the electric strike (Install the Diode at the Electric Strike; The stripe goes towards the positive wire).
  * Place the supplied jumper on BUS and NEGATIVE.![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/62f13aa0ffff0c4e1783fb8f/file-ZtHFaekfi2.png)

# Access Control: Open/Close Command

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14011565230999-Access-Control-Open-Close-Command
**Article ID:** 14011565230999

---

On the _Gate Setup_ page, you have the option to Open or Close your gate from your Storable Easy software.

Go to your Gate Settings page under **Setup > Gate.**

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5ce6b00d0428635343993151/file-Ngz0EiSFPF.png)

Clicking **Open** will be immediate, but clicking close it will go off of your gates timer to close setting. (If your gate timer is set to 30 seconds it will revert to that time.) 

**NOTE:** When using the open command from this page, it will hold the gate open until you click the close command. If you open in the the software, you should close in the software.

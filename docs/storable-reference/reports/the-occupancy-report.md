# The Occupancy Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18028197258007-The-Occupancy-Report
**Article ID:** 18028197258007

---

The Occupancy Report shows occupancy currently.

To access the report:

1\. Log in to your account.  
2\. Click the _Reports_ tab.

![Capture102.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14989d042863319924a865/Capture102.PNG)

3\. Click **Occupancy** under the Accounting/Financials column.

The statistics include unavailable units in the calculations. Also, as with other reports, this one can be printed or downloaded using the buttons in the top right corner. ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c9945472c7d3a1544615063/file-BDl9ViGgzI.png)

  * Economic occupancy is calculated by dividing the rental's current rate into the corresponding unit type's price. Products and fees are not included.
  * Area occupancy is calculated by dividing the occupied square footage into the total square footage, including units marked as Unavailable.

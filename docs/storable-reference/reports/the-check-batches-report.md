# The Check Batches Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18027582506263-The-Check-Batches-Report
**Article ID:** 18027582506263

---

The Check Batch Report logs all check and cash payments and, when printed, is essentially a large deposit slip you can take to the bank with your checks. This article will go over how to create a check batch and also how to use/print a check batch.

**Note:****If a check batch has not been created, the system will not allow you to enter check payments (checks will not be an option when you go to make a payment).**

## Creating a Check Batch

1\. Log in to your Storable Easy software.

2\. Hover over the **Reports** tab and select **All Reports**(you could also select Accounting/Financials).

![all reports.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977123566487)

3\. Under the Accounting/Financials section, select **Check Batches**.

![check batches.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977086736791)

4\. Click the **Create New Check Batch** button in the top right corner of the page.

![create new check batch.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977086739095)

5\. Enter a name for your check batch. If you take your checks to the bank once a month, you might consider making the name the current month, as shown below. Click **Create Check Batch.**

![4.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14986f042863319924a820/4.png)

6\. On the check batch report page, you will now see the check batch you just created. This page shows you when the batch was created, how many checks are currently in the batch, whether the batch is open or closed (open means you haven't deposited the checks yet), and gives you an option to edit or delete the check batch.

![Screenshot 2024-07-17 at 4.00.09 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977545795735)

You can create more than one check batch. If you want checks separated for different deposit slips, you can consider having more than one batch open at a time.

## Add checks to a Check Batch

1\. After creating a check batch, you can now begin taking check payments and log them in the check batch. To do this, first go to a customer's page, and click the **Make a Payment** button at the top.

![make payment.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977123580055)

2\. Click the desired payment button (prepay or make a payment). Under the **Payment Method** , select check. You can then enter the check number. Notice that below it, the **check batch** is automatically selected. If you have more than one check batch open, you can use the drop-down box to select a different batch.

![check batch step 2 - Lindsey Cosson.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977086749335)

## View or print a Check Batch

1\. If you return to the **Check Batch** report (see above for instructions) and click the batch name, you can view the details of the checks associated with that batch. You can also change which batch the check is associated with (if you put it in the wrong one).

![batch name.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977500859031)

2\. When you are ready to take your checks to the bank, click the **PDF** button in the top right corner of the page. 

![step 3 second - Lindsey Cosson.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977123585431)

3\. The check batch will be opened in a new screen as a deposit slip. Print this and take it with you when you go to the bank. 

![image \(52\).png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24674586468119)

## Close a Check Batch

1\. After depositing your checks, you will want to close the check batch and start a new one. To do that, go back to the check batch report and click **Close**.

![close.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977500863767)

2\. The batch will disappear from the main page. If you need to access it again (or any past check batches), click the **Closed Check Batches** button in the top right of the page.

![closed check batches.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977447263255)

From this new screen, you can re-print old check batches. You can also re-open a batch if you closed one prematurely.

![Screenshot 2024-07-17 at 4.05.03 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/24977532127895)

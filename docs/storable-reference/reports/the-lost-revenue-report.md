# The Lost Revenue Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18027980227223-The-Lost-Revenue-Report
**Article ID:** 18027980227223

---

The Lost Revenue Report shows all current empty units, the prices associated with each unit, and the total amount at the bottom of lost revenue resulting from having these units empty. Follow the steps below to locate this report:   
  
1\. Log in to your account.

2\. Click the **Reports** tab.

![Capture102.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14989d042863319924a865/Capture102.PNG)

3\. Click **Lost Revenue** under the Accounting/Financials column.

![Capture108.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14989d042863319924a866/Capture108.png)

You can sort by Unit Name or Unit Type. Select your option and click the **Sort** button

![Capture109.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14989e2c7d3a272c0ddb4c/Capture109.PNG)

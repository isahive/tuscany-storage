# The Occupancy History Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18028078519063-The-Occupancy-History-Report
**Article ID:** 18028078519063

---

The Occupancy History Report is not a historical rent roll. Rather, the report is designed to give a statistical break down of percentage occupancy so owners can see at a glance how their facility is performing.

1\. Log in to your account.

2\. Click the **Reports** tab.

![Capture102.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14989d042863319924a865/Capture102.PNG)

3\. Click **Occupancy History** under the Accounting/Financials column.

Pay attention to the italicized text above each table. The italicized text explains how the numbers are generated. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5c992b5e2c7d3a1544614f1f/file-lm59pgFYDc.png)

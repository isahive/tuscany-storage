# The Revenues Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18028323674135-The-Revenues-Report
**Article ID:** 18028323674135

---

The Revenues Report shows all payments made in the current month including past due payments but not including invoices that were invoiced and NOT paid. Follow the steps below to get to the Revenues report:

1\. Log in to your account.

2\. Click the **Reports** tab.

![Capture102.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af56042863319924a999/Capture102.PNG)

3\. Click **Revenues** under the Accounting/Financials column.

![Capture113.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af56042863319924a99a/Capture113.png)

4\. Select the date range that you want to look at.

5\. Click the **Load Report** button.

![Capture114.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14af572c7d3a272c0ddc82/Capture114.PNG)

Follow the steps below to look at revenues by unit type:

1\. Click the _Grouped by_ dropdown _._

2\. Select **Unit Type** in the dropdown.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a25da792c7d3a1a640c9435/file-H2unRcJa0v.jpg)

3\. Click the **Load Report** button.

4\. You will see the breakdown by unit types. 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a25da2e2c7d3a1a640c9432/file-napjkHXdeW.png)

### Comparing Revenues vs Future Revenues:**  
**

Revenues shows all payments made in current month including past due payments but not including invoices that were invoiced and NOT paid. 

Future revenues only shows invoices for the month so it wouldn't take into effect any past due amounts paid or if people became past due.

The ONLY time you would ever compare these reports would be if you have everyone current and the next month everyone paid you on time. So not really a good comparison.

# The Yearly Revenues Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18028665363863-The-Yearly-Revenues-Report
**Article ID:** 18028665363863

---

The Yearly Revenues Report is designed to show you information that can help you determine trends for your facility.

Follow the steps below to navigate to this report:

1\. Log in to your account.

2\. Click the **Reports** tab.

![Capture102.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a149897042863319924a856/Capture102.PNG)

3\. Click **Yearly Revenues** under the Accounting/Financials column.

![Capture112.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a149898042863319924a857/Capture112.PNG)

4\. Select the year you would like to view and click the **Load Report** button.

![Capture117.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a149898042863319924a858/Capture117.PNG)

_Note:_ Voids and Credit without payments will not update the Due and Collected totals.

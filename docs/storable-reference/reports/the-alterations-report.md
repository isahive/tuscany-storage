# The Alterations Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18027392560279-The-Alterations-Report
**Article ID:** 18027392560279

---

The Alterations Report tracks any changes made in the software and also shows who made them. For example, if a manager makes a change to an invoice amount, payment amount, or late fee, the Alterations Report will track that change. 

**1.** Log in to your Storable Easy software.

**2.** Hover over the **Reports** tab and select **All Reports**.

![Reports_Drop_Down.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1498982c7d3a272c0ddb48/Reports_Drop_Down.JPG)

**3.** Click the **Alterations** report under the Accounting/Financials column.

![2.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a149899042863319924a85a/2.png)

**4.** Input your desired date range, and click the **Load Report** button.

![3.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a149899042863319924a85b/3.JPG)

**5.** As you see below, the report shows the date of the change, to which customer it applied, what change was made (with before and after amounts), and the username of the manager who did it.

![3_-_Copy.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a149899042863319924a85c/3_-_Copy.JPG)

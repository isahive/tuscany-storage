# The Unsigned Insurance Election Agreements Report

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18030260608919-The-Unsigned-Insurance-Election-Agreements-Report
**Article ID:** 18030260608919

---

⚠️ This report will ONLY be visible if you are partnered with StorSmart Insurance and are integrated through our system.

To view the report:

1\. Log in to your account.

2\. Click the **Reports** tab.

![Capture102.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a14989d042863319924a865/Capture102.PNG)

3) Click **Unsigned Insurance Election Agreements** under the _Facility_ column.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bc7a12a042863158cc78f41/file-hcVpRxR47t.png)

The report will include any customers who have NOT signed the insurance contract.

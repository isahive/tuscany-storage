# How do I share insurance reports with a third-party insurance provider?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/29722162076695-How-do-I-share-insurance-reports-with-a-third-party-insurance-provider
**Article ID:** 29722162076695

---

If you use a third-party insurance provider, you can easily export relevant reports from Storable Easy to share data about insurance activity with them.

The **Insurance Report** is commonly shared with insurance providers. This report shows active insurance policies within the selected date range, including the Unit Number, Tenant Name, Tenant Address, Insurance Enrollment Date, Cancel Date (if applicable), Amount of Coverage, Amount Billed, Amount Collected, and Paid Through Date.

## How do I export the Insurance Report?

### Manually export the report

Follow the instructions below to export the Insurance Report.

1\. Hover over the _Reports_ tab and click **All Reports** or **Facility**. | ![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29722134523671)  
---|---  
2\. Click **Insurance.** | ![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29722162061335)  
3\. Select a date range you want included in the report and click **Load Report.** | ![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29722134533143)  
4\. Click **Export CSV** or **PDF** from the top right corner of the screen, depending on your preferred file format.  | ![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29722162065303)  
5\. Send the exported report to your insurance company.   
  
### Automatically send reports to your insurance provider

Follow the instructions below if you want insurance reports to be automatically sent to your third-party insurance provider.

1\. Hover over the _Setup_ tab and click **Insurance**. | ![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29722134539159)  
---|---  
2\. Enter your insurance provider’s email address in the email address field under Report Settings.  3\. Click **Update Site**. | ![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/29722134541207)

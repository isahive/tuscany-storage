# How do I take a payment?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17975765764887-How-do-I-take-a-payment
**Article ID:** 17975765764887

---

You can apply a payment to a tenant’s account from the Dashboard Menu or directly on the Tenant’s Account. The Dashboard _Make a Payment_ tab allows you to enter multiple payments in a row (such as a stack of cash or check payments) very quickly into the system without navigating to multiple tenant account.

## From the customer account

To make a payment to a specific customer, click on the _Customers_ tab. Search for the specific customer and click on their name. Click on the **Make a Payment** tab. This will bring up any line items that the customer owes.

![Capture91.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f4d6a2c7d3a272c0e1062/Capture91.PNG)You can make a payment for a specific line-item by clicking on the corresponding _Pay_ link to the right of the line item.

To make a payment for multiple line items, click **Make a Payment** under _Payment for Multiple Items._ You will have the option to make a payment using cash, credit card, or check. When you make a payment it will be applied to the oldest outstanding line item first and then move to the next line item due. 

## From the Dashboard drop down menu

1\. Hover your mouse over the _Dashboard_ tab in the upper right hand corner of your page, and select **Make Customer Payment** from the drop down.  
![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/641dcfb34627a93f4d0e793d/file-xhqTSr8ALj.png)

![](blob:https://sparefoot.atlassian.net/bc7a1d13-1a91-409f-9568-333f44fc63b8#media-blob-url=true&id=b4e845f8-131c-4934-a622-0736373bb583&collection=contentId-3827236957&contextId=3827236957&width=1927&height=442&alt=)

2\. From this page, you can use the dropdown list of tenants to find and highlight the name of the tenant you would like to apply the payment to, or you can use the search bar to search for the name or unit number and select the correct tenant.

3\. Once the tenant is selected, it will take you to their _Make a Payment_ page.

4\. On this page, you will see a summary of the line items due on this tenant’s account. You can either enter the amount of the payment that is being applied to their account, OR use the check boxes to the left to select specific invoices and have the amount automatically calculate for you in the _Payment Amount_ field. Once the amount is listed correctly, click **Proceed to Payment Summary** _._![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/641dcfba9a0fe82b2d574fbd/file-ViMJmpB8jw.png)

![](blob:https://sparefoot.atlassian.net/07893e46-ddcb-4e3a-a0b9-45eeb64ccfaa#media-blob-url=true&id=fd85760d-98c7-4d26-a4be-489e8842e09f&collection=contentId-3827236957&contextId=3827236957&width=1919&height=673&alt=)

5\. On the Payment Summary, you will choose the correct _Payment Method_ from the drop down menu, and enter any payment details (such as Credit card number for card payments, or account and routing number for ACH payments). If you need to adjust the amount of the payment, you will need to go back to the previous page or click **Adjust Payment Amount** _._

If the _Cash_ and _Check_ options are not showing up, this is due to a _Check Batch_ report not having an open batch to receive and track new manual payments. Learn more in our article: [The Check Batches Report. ](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18027582506263-The-Check-Batches-Report)  
![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/641dcfc51b1cd16ee8af9fbd/file-6nKWUfmW66.png)

![](blob:https://sparefoot.atlassian.net/04c0afc6-8b5a-4659-8c3f-20a780cb3e94#media-blob-url=true&id=58905ec4-9f06-41df-ac40-3a75879d885d&collection=contentId-3827236957&contextId=3827236957&width=1569&height=875&alt=)

6\. Once you have entered your payment details, click **Make a Payment** to submit the payment. Successful payments can be viewed on the Revenues Report.

7\. Once you have finished entering the payment, it will automatically redirect you back to the payment search page where you can search and select the next tenant to restart this process with the next payment.

## Take a payment with a card swiper

If the [setting to allow USB card swiper](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14499863473815-Optional-Customer-Settings) use is enabled for your facility, you can populate the credit card number and expiration date of the credit card using a card swiper by following the instructions below. _Please note:_ The credit card brand and CVV will need to be entered manually.

1\. Select **USB card swiper** as the _Payment Method_.

2\. Ensure the USB card swiper is connected to your computer, then click **Use Card Swiper**.![card swiper.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/31236385015831)

3\. The system will display "Awaiting card swipe" - your customer can now swipe their card.

4\. Complete the transaction as usual in Easy.

# Adjusting Taxes on a Tenant's Record

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/34217142907415-Adjusting-Taxes-on-a-Tenant-s-Record
**Article ID:** 34217142907415

---

There may be instances where you are required to adjust taxes on a tenant's record. To adjust the tax rate on a unit type, please see our [help article](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14503789578263-Setting-a-Tax-Rate).

To adjust taxes on a tenant's record, navigate to **Customers > All Customers**.

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34217142889111)

Select the tenant record

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34217142891927)

Click **Edit** next to the billing for each unit.

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34217142896407)

Select your new [Tax Rate](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14503789578263-Setting-a-Tax-Rate). If you don't see the tax rate available, you may need to add it to your software. Follow our [Setting a Tax Rate](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14503789578263-Setting-a-Tax-Rate) article for instructions.

![Screenshot 2025-08-27 at 1.48.52 PM.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34496616502039)

Click **Update Rental** to apply the updated tax rate for your tenant. 

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/34217149374999)

**Note** : You will need to repeat these steps to update the tax rate for every unit your tenant is currently renting.

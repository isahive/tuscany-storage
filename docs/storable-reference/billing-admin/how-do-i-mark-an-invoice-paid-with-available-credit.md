# How do I mark an invoice paid with available credit?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/16794289278103-How-do-I-mark-an-invoice-paid-with-available-credit
**Article ID:** 16794289278103

---

Occasionally, an invoice may not be properly marked as paid on a customer account when a payment is made. If that happens, the customer will have an account balance that looks like this: 

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5d31fab62c7d3a2ec4bf2004/file-qiQ9ze5irx.png)This tells us that the invoice did not get marked as paid by the available funds on their account. This usually happens if a payment was edited or removed from the customer's billing history, or if the payment was applied to an account before any units were rented to the customer. 

##  **_  
_**To mark the invoice as paid with available credit:

1\. On the customer's account, click **Make a payment.**

2\. Make sure the option _Apply Credit to Amount Due_ is selected. Leave the _Payment Amount_ As $0.00 if you have enough credit to mark the full amount of the invoice as paid and proceed to payment summary.  
  
Note: If you do not have enough credit, you will need to choose a secondary payment method to pay the difference, or wait until the customer makes an additional payment and have enough to cover the full amount of the invoice.   
  
3\. Click **Apply credit.**

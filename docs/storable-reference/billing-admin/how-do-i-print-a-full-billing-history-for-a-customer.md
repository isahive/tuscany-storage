# How do I print a full billing history for a customer?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17804068482967-How-do-I-print-a-full-billing-history-for-a-customer
**Article ID:** 17804068482967

---

To print a full billing history for a customer, go to the Customer's profile.

1\. Scroll down and click on **Full History** under the _Billing History_ section.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/6494cb6c885de842a5d8cada/file-gGkAWhUnnl.png)

2\. Click **PDF** in the upper right-hand corner and it will generate a PDF of the client's full billing history to show charges and payments.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/6494cb78046c1478e8c7715a/file-ypMppcESMB.png)

3\. Download the PDF and print it out.

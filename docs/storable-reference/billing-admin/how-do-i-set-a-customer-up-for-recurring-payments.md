# How do I set a customer up for recurring payments?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15694061277719-How-do-I-set-a-customer-up-for-recurring-payments
**Article ID:** 15694061277719

---

Follow the steps below to set your customer up with recurring billing.

1\. Go to customer's account.

2\. Click **Recurring Billing** at the top. 

3\. Click **Add Payment Account.**

Note: You can also [offset the billing date](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15486009551127-Offsetting-the-billing-date-for-a-customer-on-recurring-billing).

![Capture16.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27cc042863319924dd2f/Capture16.PNG)

4) Select **ACH** or **Credit Card** from the _Account Type_ dropdown menu.![Capture17.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27cc042863319924dd30/Capture17.PNG)

5) Fill in the customer's payment information and click **Save**.![Capture18.PNG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f27cc2c7d3a272c0e0e7b/Capture18.PNG)

⚠️ Fees for failed ACH payments are not added to tenant accounts retroactively, however, you can [add fees manually](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14500439732119-How-do-I-manually-charge-a-fee). Accounts will not be locked out automatically, but you can[ lock a tenant out manually](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14502628172951-Lockout-Settings) if needed.

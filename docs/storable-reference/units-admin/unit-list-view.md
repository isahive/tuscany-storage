# Unit List View

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14871969658647-Unit-List-View
**Article ID:** 14871969658647

---

The list view shows your units and customers by unit number. You can see the unit number, status of unit, unit type, customer name, customer phone number, customer email address, and the balance of the account. You can format the list to print or download a CSV file.

Follow the steps below to access this view:

1\. Log in to your Storable Easy account.

2\. Hover over the **Units** tab and click **List View**.

![1.JPG](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f278a042863319924dcd2/1.JPG)

3\. Customers are listed by unit number by default. You can filter the list for customer status by selecting a status from the dropdown list and clicking **Filter**. ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bbe47172c7d3a04dd5b84bd/file-O7hnsuBv9n.png)

Click on a unit number to see details of the unit or to rent it to a customer.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5bbe48932c7d3a04dd5b84d6/file-FBm1IaSzRY.png)

The list can be printed or downloaded by clicking the buttons in the top right corner of the list view page.

![5.png](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5a0382240428633199244fa0/images/5a1f278c042863319924dcd5/5.png)

**Note:** If the customer has more than one unit, each unit will show the total balance on the customer's account. For example, if a customer has rented units 2 & 3 and has a balance of $60.00, both units will show $60.00. This means the customer owes a total of $60.00.

# How do I change the status of a unit?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/33330447209879-How-do-I-change-the-status-of-a-unit
**Article ID:** 33330447209879

---

Units can have a status of _Available or Unavailable._

  * **Available** : The unit is ready to rent.
  * **Unavailable** _**:**_ The unit cannot be rented or assigned to a customer by a manager or online. 



Follow the instructions below to change the status of a unit.

1\. Log in to your Storable Easy account.

2\. Hover over the **Units** tab and click **List View**.

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/33330447203735)

3\. Click on a unit.

4\. Click **Change Status.**

**![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/33330479050903)**

5\. Select**Available** or **Unavailable.**

**![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/33330447205911)**

6\. Click**Update Status**.

![](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/33330479054359)

# Changing the size/Unit Type of a unit

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/20470433795863-Changing-the-size-Unit-Type-of-a-unit
**Article ID:** 20470433795863

---

Follow the instructions below to change the size of a unit.

1\. Navigate to **Unit > List **or**Grid View**.

2\. Click on the unit number from the Site Map, Units List, or the customer's profile. 

3\. Click **Edit** in the upper right hand corner of the page.

![edit unit.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/20470433784343)

4\. Click the dropdown menu under **Unit Type.**

**![unit type.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/20470433788311)**

5\. Change the unit type to the size you wish to change it to, then click **Update Unit.**

6\. The unit will then need to be resized on the [Site Map](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14509987325079-How-do-I-create-or-edit-my-site-map) by clicking **Edit Layout** , clicking on the unit, and selecting the **Reset Size** icon. Adjust the unit by clicking and dragging the size and click **Save**.

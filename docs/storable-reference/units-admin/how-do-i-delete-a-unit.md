# How do I delete a unit?

**Source:** https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14878567671319-How-do-I-delete-a-unit
**Article ID:** 14878567671319

---

Follow the instructions below if you need to delete a unit.

_Note:_ You can only delete a unit that is not rented or reserved. You cannot delete more than one unit at a time. 

1\. Hover over the **Units** tab and select **Grid View** or**List View.**

**![view_units.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/14878463115287)**

2\. Click on the unit number/name you want to delete.

3\. Click the **Delete** button. 

![delete_unit.png](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/14878374099223)

You will be asked to confirm that you want to delete the unit. You will not be able to restore the unit once you have deleted it. If you delete a unit accidentally, you will need to [create the unit](https://storageunitsoftware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14873188711447-How-do-I-create-a-unit-) from scratch.
